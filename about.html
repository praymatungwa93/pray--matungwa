About Me

Welcome to my personal story and journey in electronics and technology.

My Background

My name is Pray Matungwa. I was born in Chanika Ward, Karagwe District, Kagera Region, Tanzania, on 22 October 2004.

My Early Interest

From a young age, I developed an interest in mechanics, electricity and technology. My curiosity became stronger after electricity from TANESCO was introduced in my village.

My Education

I started my education at Kakiro Primary School, located in Karagwe District.

My Interest in Electronics

My interest in electronics and technology has continued to grow. I enjoy learning how electronic circuits work and developing practical electronics projects.

My Journey

This website is a place where I share my background, electronics projects, circuit designs, ideas and other technology-related work.
